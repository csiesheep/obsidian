# 隱私權政策補充草稿（2026-10-19）

給 owner 貼到 https://csiesheep.com/privacy-policy/ 的「2000：總統的誕生」一段。政策在主站，不在遊戲的 repo；這份是 orchestrator 依程式現況（main 03dfdbe，#99、#101 上線之後）整理的事實，措辭請 owner 定。

*Not legal advice.*

## 中文

**2000：總統的誕生（games.csiesheep.com/president_2000）**

- **單人遊戲。** 進度存在你這個分頁的瀏覽器裡（sessionStorage），關掉分頁就沒有了。設定（對手速度、音效、軍師、提示）和本機戰績存在你的瀏覽器裡（localStorage），不會送到伺服器。
- **多人房間。** 你輸入的名字、你的座位、牌局和最近 50 句聊天，存在伺服器上那間房裡。最後一個人離線 30 分鐘後，整間房連同這些資料一起刪除。你的瀏覽器會記住這間房的座位憑證（token）：分頁裡一份；另一份最多 24 小時（從最後一次連上算），離開房間時刪除。
- **防止濫用。** 為了擋住逐一猜房間代碼和大量開房，伺服器會依你的連線位址（IPv6 以 /64 為一組）記下最近 10 分鐘內加入失敗與開房的時間。只記時間，不記內容；10 分鐘內沒有新紀錄，就整筆刪除。
- **主機與紀錄。** 網站由 Cloudflare 提供，Cloudflare 會處理你的連線位址。網站開啟了 Cloudflare Workers 的請求紀錄（網址、時間、回應狀態）；加入房間時，你的名字和座位憑證不放在網址裡，不會出現在這份紀錄裡。
- **字型。** 頁面從 Google Fonts 載入部分字型，你的瀏覽器會向 Google 送出請求（含你的連線位址）。

## English

**2000: Taiwan Decides (games.csiesheep.com/president_2000)**

- **Solo games.** Your progress is kept in this browser tab (sessionStorage) and is gone when you close the tab. Settings (rivals' speed, sound, the advisor, tips) and your local record are kept in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to the server.
- **Rooms.** The name you type, your seat, the game and the last 50 chat lines are kept on the server in that room. 30 minutes after the last player leaves, the room and all of this are deleted. Your browser keeps the room's seat token: one copy in the tab, another for at most 24 hours (counted from your last connection), removed when you leave the room.
- **Preventing abuse.** To stop room codes being guessed one by one and rooms being opened in bulk, the server records, per connecting address (IPv6 by its /64), the times of failed joins and of rooms opened in the last 10 minutes. Only times are kept, nothing else; a record with nothing new for 10 minutes is deleted.
- **Hosting and logs.** The site runs on Cloudflare, which processes your connecting address. Request logging for Cloudflare Workers is on (addresses, times, response status); when you join a room, your name and seat token are not in the address, so they do not appear in those logs.
- **Fonts.** The pages load some fonts from Google Fonts; your browser sends a request to Google (including your connecting address).

## orchestrator 的備註

- 「名字與 token 在網址裡、會進 Workers 的紀錄」是盤點 E 第 5 條，#105 上線（0c81e8e，Version 6bb38fd6）之後已不成立，第四點已改寫。正式環境的紀錄裡真的沒有，要 owner 在 Cloudflare 儀表板看一眼才算確認。
