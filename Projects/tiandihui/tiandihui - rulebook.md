# tiandihui — rulebook digest

Digest, in my own words, of the base rules plus the expansions of the game
the play is modelled on, for [[tiandihui plan]]. Kept as a mechanics
reference only. The site ships under its own name and setting (天地會),
and its rules page is written fresh from this, never from the
publisher's prose. Faction words here are the original's; the site's are
兄弟 / 密探.

Designer Don Eskridge; publisher Indie Boards & Cards. 5–10 players, ~30
min. 1st ed. 2009/2010; 2nd ed. added Plot cards; 3rd ed. dropped Plot
cards and added the Inquisitor; *Hidden Agenda* and *Hostile Intent*
expansions add the modules listed at the end.

## Components (physical)
10 character cards (operatives / spies), 5 mission-card sets (Success +
Fail), 5 team tokens, 20 vote tokens (Approve / Reject per player), 5
score markers (blue = success, red = fail), round marker, vote-track
marker, leader token, score tableaus per player count.

## Base game

### Setup
| Players | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operatives | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 6 |
| Spies | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 |

- Deal one character card each, face down. Nobody may ever show their
  card.
- Random first leader.
- **Spies reveal**: everyone closes eyes; spies open and identify each
  other; spies close; everyone opens. Spies know each other; operatives
  know nothing.

### Round = team building + mission
1. **Propose.** Leader assigns exactly N team tokens (table below), may
   include themself, one per player. Open discussion at any time.
2. **Vote.** Everyone, leader included, secretly picks Approve/Reject;
   all revealed at once (votes are public).
   Majority approve ⇒ mission. Tie or majority reject ⇒ rejected, leader
   passes clockwise, propose again.
   **Fifth rejection in one round ⇒ spies win immediately.**
3. **Mission.** Each team member secretly plays Success or Fail.
   Operatives *must* play Success; spies may play either. Cards are
   shuffled before reveal so nobody knows who played what.
   Any Fail ⇒ mission fails. **Exception: 4th mission with 7+ players
   needs two Fails.**
4. Mark the mission blue/red, advance the round marker, leader passes
   clockwise.

| Mission | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 2nd | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 3rd | 2 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 4th | 3 | 3 | 4* | 5* | 5* | 5* |
| 5th | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 |

\* two Fails needed.

### End
Immediately at three successes (operatives win) or three fails (spies
win), or five rejected proposals in one round (spies win).

### Official variants
- **Targeting** — leader also chooses *which* mission (any unattempted
  one, team size follows the mission); the vote covers mission + team;
  the 5th mission is locked until two others have succeeded.
- **Blind Spies** — skip the reveal; spies don't know each other.

## The Plot Thickens (2nd-edition expansion, 15 plot cards)
Plot cards are public. At the start of each round the leader draws
1 (5–6 players) / 2 (7–8) / 3 (9–10) and hands each to a *different*
player, never themself. 5–6 players use only 7 of the 15 cards.
Three kinds: ★ use immediately; ① hold until used; □ permanent. When
several could apply, order is clockwise from the leader; each player
gets one chance and can't pass then play.

| Card | × | Kind | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overheard Conversation | 2 | ★ | Look at an adjacent player's character card |
| Opinion Maker | 2 | □ | Must vote first, openly, every vote for the rest of the game |
| Establish Confidence | 1 | ★ | Leader shows their own character card to one player |
| Open Up | 1 | ★ | Holder shows their character card to one player of their choice |
| No Confidence | 3 | ① | Cancel an approved vote; counts as a rejection on the track |
| Keeping a Close Eye on You | 2 | ① | Secretly look at one played mission card (one checker per player per round) |
| Strong Leader | 2 | ① | Take the leader token before the leader acts; not twice between votes |
| In the Spotlight | 1 | ① | Force one team member to play their mission card face up; declare before cards are chosen |
| Take Responsibility | 1 | ① | Take one plot card from any player |

## Modules (Hidden Agenda / Hostile Intent)
Each is an optional overlay on the base game; the base loop and win
conditions stay.
- **Assassin** (the Avalon set): *Commander* knows the spies; if three
  missions succeed the *Assassin* names one player, and the spies win if
  it's the Commander. Optional powers: *Body Guard* (knows the
  Commander), *Deep Cover* (spy hidden from the Commander), *Blind Spy*
  (unknown to the other spies and doesn't know them), *False Commander*
  (looks like the Commander to the Body Guard). Avalon names: Merlin /
  Assassin / Percival / Mordred / Oberon / Morgana.
- **Trapper**: teams are one bigger; leader looks at one played card and
  discards it before the shuffle.
- **Defector**: one operative and one spy are Defectors; from round 3 a
  deck may secretly swap their allegiances.
- **Rogue Agent**: a rogue on either side wins alone by playing their
  special card on the decisive mission and once before; a Watch token
  blocks it.
- **Sergeant**: a team member chosen by the leader may swap one other
  player's played card before the shuffle and sees what it was.
- **Inquisitor**: after missions 2, 3 and 4 the token holder inspects one
  player's card, then passes the token to them.
- **Reverser**: a Reverse card flips the mission result (two cancel).
- **Hunter**: each side has a Chief; the opposing Hunter must accuse the
  right Chief after the third success/fail to secure the win.

Recommendation in the rules themselves: learn the base game first, add
one thing at a time. v1 of the site is the base game only.
